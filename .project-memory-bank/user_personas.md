# User Personas

Personas anchor every screen and journey. Each later screen must serve at least one
persona's documented journey ([`user_journeys.md`](user_journeys.md)).

---

## P1 — New Engineer (Onboarding)

**Goal:** Become productive quickly; understand systems, ownership, and "why".
**Pains:** Tribal knowledge, scattered docs, fear of asking "obvious" questions,
slow ramp.
**EKIP value:** Fast, trustworthy answers about how services work and who owns them.

## P2 — Senior / Staff Engineer

**Goal:** Make sound changes without breaking things; understand dependencies and
historical decisions.
**Pains:** Architectural drift, hidden dependencies, lost rationale behind past
decisions.
**EKIP value:** Dependency maps, ADR/context recall, impact analysis.

## P3 — Engineering Manager / Tech Lead

**Goal:** Understand ownership, risk, and knowledge health across the team.
**Pains:** Bus-factor risk, unclear ownership, repeated incidents, onboarding cost.
**EKIP value:** Ownership clarity, knowledge coverage, risk visibility.

## P4 — SRE / On-call Engineer

**Goal:** Resolve incidents fast; understand blast radius and prior similar incidents.
**Pains:** Time pressure, unknown dependencies, scattered incident history.
**EKIP value:** Incident exploration, impacted-service maps, prior resolutions.

## P5 — Platform / Architecture Owner

**Goal:** Keep the system coherent; track tech debt and dependency risk org-wide.
**Pains:** Drift, accumulating debt, fragile dependency graphs.
**EKIP value:** Intelligence dashboards (debt, dependency risk), architecture
explanations.

See [`user_journeys.md`](user_journeys.md).
